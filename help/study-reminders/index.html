How to set a daily study reminder in Yaaddi

Short answerIn Settings, under Reminders, switch on Daily study reminder and choose a time. Yaaddi nudges you at that time on days you have not studied yet.

Settings · Last updated 2026-11-18 · For the Yaaddi Android app

Steps

  1. Open the Settings tab and find Reminders.

  2. Switch on Daily study reminder. If your phone asks to allow notifications, allow them.

  3. Set the Reminder time. Tap Suggest a time from my history to let the app pick a time you usually study.

Good to know

  • The reminder may arrive a few minutes late, and it is skipped on days you have already studied.
  • If you stay away, reminders continue for up to a week and then stop. Opening the app re-plans them.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server and the app uses no push service.
  • If you see Notifications are blocked, allow notifications for Yaaddi in your phone's settings.
  • Suggest a time needs some study history first. Before that, the app tells you there is not enough yet.

Common questions

Why am I not getting reminders?

Check that the switch is on, that notifications are allowed for Yaaddi in your phone's settings, and that your phone is not stopping the app in the background with battery saving.

Does the reminder know how many cards are due?

Yes. When cards are due, the notification says how many; otherwise it shows a short encouragement.
