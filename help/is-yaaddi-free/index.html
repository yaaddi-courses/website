Is Yaaddi free?

Short answerYes. Yaaddi is free on Google Play, has no ads and needs no account. The courses in the Course Library are free too.

Get started · Last updated 2026-11-18 · For the Yaaddi Android app

What you get

  • The Yaaddi app, free to download from Google Play
  • Every course in the in-app Course Library, which you can also browse on courses.yaaddi.com
  • No ads and no account to create

Good to know

  • Yaaddi runs on Android. It is available from Google Play.
  • Your courses and progress are stored on your phone. See Where does Yaaddi store my data?
