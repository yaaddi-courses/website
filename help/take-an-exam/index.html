How to take a practice exam in Yaaddi

Short answerOpen a course and tap Test yourself. Choose what to be tested on, the number of questions and an optional timer. You see your score, a per-deck breakdown and every correct answer only after you submit.

Study · Last updated 2026-11-18 · For the Yaaddi Android app

Steps

  1. Open a course from the Learn tab and tap Test yourself.

  2. Pick the exam. A course can offer an Official exam format (a fixed number of questions, time limit and pass mark), numbered exams, or a Random mix.

  3. Under What should you be tested on?, choose Whole course, Decks I've studied or Choose decks.

  4. For a practice exam, set the number of questions and the time limit, or turn the timer off. Tap Start exam.

  5. Answer one question at a time. Use Flag for review to mark a question and Questions to jump around. Tap Submit exam when you are done.

What you see afterwards

The results screen shows pass or not passed, your score, a breakdown by deck, and a review of every question with the right answer and the author's explanation. Practice my mistakes starts an untimed exam made from the questions you got wrong.

Good to know

  • During an exam there is no feedback: no colors, sounds or explanations, so it feels like the real thing. You can change answers until you submit.
  • The exam is saved as you go, and the clock does not stop if you close the app. Reopen Test yourself to Resume exam or Discard it.
  • Exams use multiple-choice and true-or-false questions, and they never change your spaced-repetition schedule.
  • Each correct answer earns one Coin when the results are shown. Nothing is taken away for a wrong or blank answer.
  • Passing the whole course at full length earns a certificate you can share. It says it is a practice exam inside Yaaddi and is not an official certificate, which matters for courses such as driving or citizenship tests.

Common questions

Why is Test yourself missing or unavailable?

A course needs multiple-choice or true-or-false questions to build an exam. Otherwise the app says This course has no multiple-choice or true/false questions to build an exam from.

Can I report a bad question?

Yes. Every question has Report a problem with this question, during the exam and in the results review.
