Where does Yaaddi store my data, and is it private?

Short answerYour courses, cards, progress, streaks, Coins and settings stay in a database on your phone. Yaaddi has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and it does not read advertising IDs.

Your data · Last updated 2026-11-18 · For the Yaaddi Android app

What stays on your phone

Everything you create or earn: courses, cards, review history, streaks, Coins, settings, and any cover images or audio you add. The developer never receives it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

What leaves your phone

  • Course Library and updates: the app downloads course lists and files from a public GitHub repository. GitHub sees your IP address and the files requested, as with any website. Yaaddi sends no identifier.
  • App updates: on Android, the app asks Google Play whether a newer version exists.
  • Report a problem: opens your email app with a message you can read before you send.
  • Sharing: Share progress sends a picture only to the app you choose.

Permissions

Notifications (optional, for study reminders) and photos (optional, for cover images). Files are read only through the system file picker. The app never asks for the camera or microphone.

Good to know

  • This describes the Yaaddi app. For the full legal text, read the Yaaddi privacy policy.
  • This website, yaaddi.com, uses optional Google Analytics only if you accept it. See Privacy and analytics.
  • Yaaddi is not directed at children under 13.

Common questions

Can you delete my data?

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage. If you emailed a report and want it deleted, write to support@yaaddi.com.
