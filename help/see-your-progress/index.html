Where to see your progress and stats in Yaaddi

Short answerOpen the Stats tab. It shows cards answered, accuracy, total Coins, days studied, your streaks and freezes, and charts of your progress over time. Each course has its own stats too.

Progress · Last updated 2026-11-18 · For the Yaaddi Android app

Steps

  1. Open the Stats tab at the bottom of the app.

  2. Switch between All time and This week at the top of the summary.

  3. Scroll to Progress over time and choose 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or All. Tap a chart to see a single day.

  4. Use the course dropdown to see the stats of one course, including its exams.

  5. Tap Share progress to send a picture of your progress and a link to the app through any app you choose.

Good to know

  • Stats count lesson answers. Exam answers appear in the Exams section instead and stay out of your accuracy.
  • On the Learn tab, the number of cards due today and the Study button show what to review next.
  • Everything is calculated on your phone. Sharing progress sends nothing by itself: you choose where the picture goes.

Common questions

Why are my stats empty?

They fill in after you answer your first cards. Install a course from the library and study a deck.
