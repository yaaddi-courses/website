Quick answers

Is Yaaddi free?

Yes. Yaaddi and its courses are free on Google Play, with no ads.

Is Yaaddi free? →

Does Yaaddi work offline?

Yes, once a course is installed. Only the library and updates need the internet.

Does Yaaddi work offline? →

How do I move Yaaddi to a new phone?

Create a backup on the old phone and restore it on the new one.

How to back up Yaaddi and move it to a new phone →

Which spaced-repetition algorithm is best?

The default suits most people. You can switch to FSRS, SM-2, ACT-R, HLR or Fibonacci.

How to change the spaced-repetition algorithm in Yaaddi →

How do I keep my streak?

Study once a day. Streak freezes and a weekly grace day cover missed days.

How streaks, streak freezes and Coins work in Yaaddi →

Can I skip what I already know?

Yes. The "What do you know?" check marks known decks so you study only what is new.
