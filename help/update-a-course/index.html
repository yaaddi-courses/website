How to update a course in Yaaddi

Short answerOpen the Course Library, find Installed courses and tap Check for updates, then Update. Progress on cards that did not change is kept.

Your data · Last updated 2026-11-18 · For the Yaaddi Android app

Steps

  1. Open the Course Library from the Learn tab.

  2. Under Installed courses, tap Check for updates. A course with news shows Update available.

  3. Tap Update. When it finishes you see Course updated: new content is in, and your review progress for anything unchanged was kept.

Good to know

  • When you open an installed course, Yaaddi also checks for a newer version, at most once a day per course. A banner says An update is available for this course with a View button.
  • If an update removes cards you have already studied, the app tells you how many and asks whether to continue, because their progress would be lost.
  • For a course you imported from a file, use Update from file in the course's menu instead.
  • To update the Yaaddi app itself, use Google Play. A red dot on the Settings tab shows when a new version is available.

Common questions

Do I lose my progress when a course updates?

Not for cards that stay in the course. Only cards the update removes lose their progress, and you are warned first.
