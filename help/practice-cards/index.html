What practice cards are and how to change them in Yaaddi

Short answerPractice cards are extra questions linked to a main card. Yaaddi shows them when you learn a card for the first time and right after a wrong answer. You can tune both in Settings → Advanced learning options.

Study · Last updated 2026-11-18 · For the Yaaddi Android app

Steps

  1. Open the Settings tab and tap Advanced learning options.

  2. Under Practice cards for new material, choose All, Adaptive or None. This only affects the first time you learn a card.

  3. With Adaptive, set Fade after: how many correct answers in a row on a card's practice exercises before the rest fade out.

  4. Under Practice cards on a wrong answer, choose how many of a missed card's practice cards are asked right after it: all of them, or a fixed number.

  5. Turn on All cards in spaced repetition if you want practice cards to be scheduled for review too, each on its own schedule.

Good to know

  • Main cards are always scheduled for review. Practice cards are scheduled only if you turn on All cards in spaced repetition.
  • Cards you are reviewing again later always show practice only after a wrong answer, whatever the new-material setting says.
  • The defaults suit most people. Change one setting at a time so you can tell what helps.

Common questions

What does "Custom" do?

Custom lets you set the exact number of practice cards to show up front, instead of all or adaptive.
