How to organize, sort and delete courses in Yaaddi

Short answerUse the three-dot menu on the Learn tab to make groups and sort courses, and the menu on each course card to move it to a group, edit it or delete it.

Get started · Last updated 2026-11-18 · For the Yaaddi Android app

Steps

  1. To make a folder, open the three-dot menu on the Learn tab and choose New group. Give it a name and tap Create.

  2. To put a course in a group, tap the three dots on the course card and choose Move to group.

  3. To change the order, choose Sort courses in the three-dot menu. A custom order lets you drag courses where you want them.

  4. To find a course quickly, type in the Find a course box at the top of the Learn tab.

  5. To remove a course, tap the three dots on its card and choose Delete.

Good to know

  • Deleting a course removes its cards and your progress on them from this phone. If you might want it back, create a backup first. See How to back up Yaaddi.
  • A course from the library can be installed again at any time, but your old progress will not come back unless you restore a backup.

Common questions

Can I put a group inside another group?

Yes. Groups can contain subgroups.
