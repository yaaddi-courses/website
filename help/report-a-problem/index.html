How to report a mistake in a Yaaddi card

Short answerWhile studying, tap the three-dot menu and choose Report a problem. Pick a reason, add a note, and Yaaddi opens your email app with the report ready to send. Nothing is sent until you press send.

Study · Last updated 2026-11-18 · For the Yaaddi Android app

Steps

  1. In a lesson, tap the three-dot button at the top right.

  2. Choose Report a problem.

  3. Pick a reason: The correct answer is wrong, Typo or unclear wording, Inappropriate content, Image or audio does not work or Something else. Add details if you like.

  4. Tap Continue. Your email app opens with the message filled in. Review it and press send.

Good to know

  • The email goes to support@yaaddi.com and includes your note, the reason, the course name and version, the card ID and type, a short excerpt of the card text, and the app version and platform. You see all of it before you send.
  • In an exam, use Report a problem with this question on the question or in the results review.
  • If no email app opens, write to support@yaaddi.com and name the course and the card.

Common questions

What happens to my report?

We use it to fix the card and to reply to you at the address you sent it from. A correction reaches you as an updated course version. See How to update a course.
