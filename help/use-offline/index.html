Does Yaaddi work offline?

Short answerYes. After a course is installed, you can study it, take exams and track progress without a connection. Only the Course Library and course and app updates need the internet.

Your data · Last updated 2026-11-18 · For the Yaaddi Android app

What works offline

  • Studying any installed course and answering every question type
  • Spaced-repetition reviews and practice cards
  • Exams and certificates
  • Stats, streaks, Coins and settings
  • Creating, editing and importing courses from files on your phone
  • Reading the privacy policy and terms inside the app

What needs a connection

  • Opening the Course Library and installing a course
  • Checking for and installing course updates
  • Checking Google Play for a new version of the app
  • Pictures in an imported course that point to a web address

Good to know

  • Install the courses you want while you are online. Then Yaaddi works on a flight, a commute or anywhere with no signal.
  • Reading aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine, so it does not need a connection either.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts. Your courses and progress are stored on your phone.
