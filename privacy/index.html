Privacy and analytics

In shortThe Yaaddi app keeps your courses and progress on your phone and has no analytics or ads. This website, yaaddi.com, can use Google Analytics, but only if you click Accept.

Last updated 2026-11-18

The Yaaddi app

Your courses, progress, streaks and settings stay on your phone, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools. The full text is in the app privacy policy. For a plain-language version, see Where does Yaaddi store my data?

This website

Your choice

If analytics is switched on for this site, a small banner asks you once. Your choice is saved in your browser, and your browser's Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal counts as Decline. Your current choice: declined.

What happens if you accept

  • The page loads Google's analytics script and sets cookies named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID.
  • Google Analytics 4 records which pages you view, how you arrived (for example from a search engine), your approximate location such as country and city, your browser and device type, and clicks on our links to Google Play, courses.yaaddi.com and support email. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses.
  • Advertising features, Google signals and ad personalization are turned off. We do not use the data for advertising and we do not sell it.
  • Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months.

What happens if you decline

Nothing is sent to Google and no Google script is loaded. The site works the same.

Why we measure

To see which help pages answer people's questions and which questions are missing, so we can improve them.

Hosting

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may process your IP address and request details to serve the pages, under GitHub's privacy statement.
