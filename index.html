Learn it once, remember it

Yaaddi is built around how memory works. You answer, the app schedules the review, and you spend your time on what you are about to forget.

Courses in order

Start at the first lesson and follow a clear path. Each course is a map of units, so you always know where you are and what comes next.

Answer, don't flip

Twenty question types, from multiple choice to put-in-order, match-the-pairs and fill-in-the-code. You test yourself instead of tapping through cards.

Reviews timed for memory

Choose the schedule that suits you: SM-2, FSRS, ACT-R, HLR or Fibonacci. Get a question wrong and related practice cards drill the idea right away.

Private and offline

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your courses and progress live on your phone, and installed courses work without a connection.

Make it yours

Create your own courses, organize them in folders, search them, and back everything up to one file.

Stay motivated

Streaks, coins and a progress dashboard keep you going, in light or dark mode.

Why Yaaddi exists

Yaaddi started as a tool for one person. Mohammad Reza Mahdiani needed to keep learning new technology for his work, and none of the apps he tried did everything he wanted: courses from zero, real questions instead of flipping cards, no ads, and no need for a connection or an account. So he built one, made a course for himself, and then shared it for free so others could have the same experience.

If you want a course on a subject that is missing, or you find a mistake, tell us. The next course could be yours.

Questions

Is Yaaddi free?

Yes. Yaaddi is free on Google Play and has no ads.

Do I need an account to use Yaaddi?

No. There are no accounts. Your courses and progress are stored on your phone.

Does Yaaddi work offline?

Yes. Once a course is installed, you can study without a connection. Only the in-app course library needs a connection, to download courses.

Which review schedules does Yaaddi support?

You can choose between SM-2, FSRS, ACT-R, HLR and Fibonacci scheduling in Settings.

Can I create my own courses?

Yes. You can create, edit and import your own courses, and back up everything to a file.

What if I find a mistake in a card?

Use Report a problem inside the app, or write to support@yaaddi.com.
